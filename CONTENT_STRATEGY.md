## AGENT_MAXXING
*Niche: AI / agents / automation / productivity / future of work*

### Content Pillars

1. **AI Tool Demos** — Short walkthroughs of new AI tools, agents, and automations. Format: screen capture + voiceover. Hook: "I tested [tool] so you don't have to."
2. **Agent Culture** — Clips and commentary on the emerging agentic AI movement. Repurpose content from AI researchers, founders, and builders on X, YouTube, podcasts.
3. **Productivity Hacks** — Workflow automations that save real time. Before/after format. Hook: "This automation saved me 4 hours."
4. **Future-of-Work Commentary** — Reaction and opinion content on AI replacing jobs, new roles, the builder economy. Opinionated takes perform best.
5. **Build Logs** — Behind-the-scenes clips of building automation pipelines and AI workflows. Authenticity driver; builds community trust.

### Posting Frequency

| Platform | Format | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| TikTok | Vertical, 30–60s, caption + hook | 1–2x/day |
| Instagram Reels | Same as TikTok, slight caption edit | 1x/day |
| YouTube Shorts | Same clips, optimized title | 5x/week |
| X | Clip link + text take | 2x/day |

### Content Transition (Gradual)

- **Month 1–2:** Pure repurposing. Clip top AI YouTube channels (Fireship, Matt Wolfe, AI Explained, Andrej Karpathy, Lex Fridman). Add captions, hook overlay, routing.
- **Month 3–4:** Add original commentary layer. Record 10–30s opinion intro before repurposed clip. Voice becomes part of the brand.
- **Month 5+:** Introduce original walkthroughs and tutorials. Repurposed content drops to 50% of output.

### Monetization / Growth Opportunities

1. **Newsletter** — Beehiiv or Substack. "Agent Maxxing Weekly" covering AI tool releases and automation wins. Upsell from every post.
2. **SaaS Affiliate Partnerships** — AI tools pay 20–40% recurring commissions. Target: Notion AI, Zapier, Make, Clay, Perplexity, ElevenLabs.
3. **Digital Products** — Prompt packs, automation templates, workflow guides. One-time purchase, low support cost.
4. **Community** — Paid Discord or Circle community for builders. $20–50/month. Content feeds the funnel; community closes it.
5. **Consulting / Advisory** — For companies wanting to implement AI agents. High-ticket. Drives from content credibility.

## AGENT_PASTFORWARD
*Niche: History / forgotten technology / old predictions / future forecasting / timelines*

### Content Pillars

1. **"They Predicted This"** — Old footage or text predictions (1950s–1990s) shown alongside the reality today. Split-screen or sequential format. Highest virality potential.
2. **Forgotten Technology** — Inventions, companies, and ideas that were ahead of their time and failed or were forgotten. Hook: "This existed in 1974."
3. **Timeline Comparisons** — Then vs. now comparisons showing acceleration of change. Format: two-panel side by side or rapid montage.
4. **Future Forecasting** — Based on historical patterns, predict what comes next. Ties past content to current relevance. "If this happened in 1990, what happens next?"
5. **Historical Turning Points** — Key decisions or events that bent the arc of technology. Storytelling format. "The day [X] almost didn't happen."

### Posting Frequency

| Platform | Format | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| TikTok | Vertical, 45–90s, narrated | 5x/week |
| Instagram Reels | Same, slightly condensed | 4x/week |
| YouTube Shorts | Full narrated format | 3x/week |
| X | Still image + text thread teaser | 3x/week |

### Content Transition (Gradual)

- **Month 1–2:** Pure archive repurposing. Pull from public domain sources (Archive.org, Prelinger Archives, Wikimedia Commons). Focus on "They Predicted This" format exclusively.
- **Month 3–4:** Add narrated voiceover layer. The voice becomes the brand. Introduce "Forgotten Tech" pillar.
- **Month 5+:** Introduce original research content. Commission or produce original comparisons. Begin "Future Forecasting" series as earned credibility grows.

### Monetization / Growth Opportunities

1. **Education Sponsorships** — Brilliant.org, MasterClass, Coursera pay premium rates for history/tech adjacent content. High CPM niche.
2. **Newsletter** — "The Past Forward Weekly" — curated predictions that came true, one forgotten invention, one forward forecast. Evergreen content.
3. **Book / Media Deals** — A sufficiently large archive and audience creates inbound interest from publishers and podcast networks.
4. **Speaking & Events** — History + future-of-tech is a conference niche. Builds naturally from content credibility.
5. **Documentary Licensing** — Original research content can be licensed to media companies or developed into long-form YouTube documentaries (higher CPM, longer shelf life).

## AGENT_PASTFORWARD — SIGNATURE CAMPAIGN: "THE FASTEST TIMELINE"

### Concept

A recurring series documenting how the pace of technological change has accelerated across every decade.

**Hook formula:** "What took [X] years in [decade] now takes [Y] months."

**Format:**
- Episode 1: Computing. 1950 → 2026. The compression of 70 years into 3-minute arc.
- Episode 2: Communication. Telegraph → smartphone. The gap between each leap shrinks.
- Episode 3: AI. 60 years of academic research → 3 years of consumer products.
- Episode N: The audience submits nominations for the next episode.

**Virality mechanic:** Each episode ends with an open question about what comes next. Audience comments complete the story. Comment engagement signals boost algorithmic reach.

**Monetization hooks:**
- Episode sponsor slot (education/tech brand, 15s mid-roll)
- Newsletter CTA in every episode (the deeper research lives in the newsletter)
- "Become a Timeline Member" — paid tier for early episode access and research notes

**Cross-promotion:** agent_maxxing shares "The Fastest Timeline: AI" episode. Builds brand awareness across the network.

**Production requirement:** Each episode requires 30–60 minutes of human research + scripting. Pipeline handles clip assembly. Not automatable at the clip level — this is the original-content tier.

## AGENT_VIRAL

### Content Pillars

4. **Trending Formats** — Adapt the current viral video format to new content. Fast-follow strategy.

| Platform | Format | Frequency |

*Highest posting frequency of all accounts — automation dependency is highest here.*

### Monetization / Growth Opportunities

1. **Ad Revenue** — Volume play. High posting frequency + broad audience = strong ad revenue at scale.
2. **Brand Deals** — Consumer brands pay for entertainment-adjacent placement. Food, gaming peripherals, streaming services.
3. **Merchandise** — Meme-adjacent products. Fast-turn drops tied to trending moments.
4. **Viral Boosting Service** — Brands pay to have their viral moment amplified. Paid promotion layer.
5. **Affiliate Links** — Amazon and product affiliate links tied to "what I used in this video" format.

## CROSS-NETWORK RULES

1. **No two active accounts post the same clip** — routing classifier enforces this in pipeline.py.
2. **Cross-promotion is contextual only** — see Section 7 of BRAND_ARCHITECTURE.md.
3. **Music copyright applies network-wide** — agent_afterhours is highest risk; flag all music clips for human review.
4. **agent_viral and agent_afterhours never share a clip** — entertainment vs. niche; audience overlap would confuse both algorithms.
5. **agent_pastforward content is evergreen** — no time pressure. Quality > speed for this account.
