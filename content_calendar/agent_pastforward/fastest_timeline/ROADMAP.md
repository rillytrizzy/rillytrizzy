## SERIES CONCEPT

Each episode documents one technology category and shows how the pace of advancement
has compressed across decades — what took 50 years once now takes 5.

**Core hook formula:** "What took [X] years in [era] now takes [Y] months."
**End-of-episode CTA:** Open question inviting audience debate.
**Monetization:** Sponsor slot between beat 3 and beat 4 of each episode.
**Newsletter:** Every episode drives to newsletter for deeper research.

## SPONSOR PLACEMENT FRAMEWORK

**Slot:** Between beat 3 (modern era) and beat 4 (compression moment) — 15 seconds.

**Why this placement works:** Audience is engaged from beats 1–3. Sponsor interrupts at
peak curiosity (they want to know the conclusion). The resolution after the sponsor rewards
staying through the ad.

**Target sponsor categories:**
1. Education platforms: Brilliant.org, MasterClass, Coursera, Skillshare
2. Research/productivity tools: Notion, Readwise, Perplexity
3. AI tools: Any tool with an education/research angle
4. Book publishers and audiobook platforms

**Sponsor script template:**
"This episode is brought to you by [SPONSOR]. [SPONSOR] helps [target audience] [do thing]
in [benefit]. [One specific feature or offer]. Try it free at [link] — link in bio."

**Rate benchmark (when monetized):**
- 1,000–10,000 followers: $25–75 per episode
- 10,000–50,000 followers: $75–300 per episode
- 50,000–100,000 followers: $300–750 per episode

## RESEARCH WORKFLOW (per episode)

```
Session 1 — Topic + Hook (15 min)
1. Open EPISODE_TEMPLATE.md for the episode number
2. Fill: Topic, Era Start, Era End
3. Find the hook: what is the most surprising fact about acceleration in this category?
4. Confirm at least 3 source clips exist (Archive.org, Wikimedia, or YouTube fair use)

Session 2 — Research + Script (30–45 min)
5. Identify 5 key milestones (one per era beat)
6. Find or confirm source clip for each milestone
7. Write narration script using EPISODE_TEMPLATE.md script section
8. Note sponsor slot placement

Session 3 — Production (30–60 min)
9. Download source clips (pipeline.py or manual yt-dlp)
10. Record narration voiceover (or generate via ElevenLabs)
11. Assemble in CapCut / Descript / DaVinci
12. Burn captions (or use pipeline.py caption output)
13. Export vertical 1080x1920

Session 4 — Review + Post
14. Fill review_queue fields manually (approved: true, series: fastest_timeline)
15. Copy to content_calendar/agent_pastforward/YYYY-MM-DD/
16. Post to TikTok → Instagram → YouTube Shorts → X thread
```
