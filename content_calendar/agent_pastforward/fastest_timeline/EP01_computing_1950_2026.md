## Key Milestones (5 beats)

1. **1951 — UNIVAC I** — The first commercially sold computer. 13 tons. Fills a room. Cost: $1M (1951). Used to predict the 1952 US election. One unit. One purpose.

2. **1971 — Intel 4004** — First microprocessor on a single chip. 2,300 transistors. The size of a fingernail. Computing shrinks from a room to a chip for the first time. The 20-year compression begins.

3. **1984 — Apple Macintosh** — First mass-market personal computer with a graphical interface. 128KB RAM. $2,495. Computers enter living rooms. Another 13 years, another leap.

4. **2007 — iPhone** — A supercomputer in a pocket. More processing power than the Apollo Guidance Computer. Networked to every other pocket in the world. The interval: 23 years. The gap is already shrinking.

5. **2022–2026 — GPT-4 → Claude 3 → Agent Networks** — AI that writes, codes, reasons, and acts autonomously. From the iPhone to AI agents: 15 years. From GPT-3 to Claude 3: 3 years. Each loop tightens.
