## Status Values
- `PENDING` — in review queue, not yet approved
- `APPROVED` — human signed off, ready to post
- `POSTED` — live on platform
- `REJECTED` — failed review (copyright, quality, routing error)
- `HELD` — approved but held for scheduling

## Copyright Check Values
- `CLEAR` — no recognizable music or licensed footage
- `MUSIC_RISK` — contains music; do not post without license confirmation
- `REVIEW` — uncertain; needs manual check before posting
